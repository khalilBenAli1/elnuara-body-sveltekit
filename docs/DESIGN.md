# Original design direction

Elnuara is a fictional November2026 body-care application with a calm sage/cream/amber identity. Its homepage is an original sculptural product poster: a central oval aperture, offset sensory rail, considered serif scale and a quiet cream frame. It does not reuse the Roam workspace, Veyli face/pigment collage, hotel split hero, accessories triptych or paired-fashion composition from the other portfolio projects.

## Primary research

Current official pages were consulted on November3,2026 for product organization and content hierarchy:

- [Salt & Stone](https://www.saltandstone.com/): clear navigation by product and scent.
- [Nécessaire](https://necessaire.com/): readable body/hand families and explicit product/size information.
- [Grown Alchemist](https://grownalchemist.com/): material-led product presentation and focused body/hand navigation.
- [SvelteKit introduction](https://svelte.dev/docs/kit/introduction), [static adapter](https://svelte.dev/docs/kit/adapter-static), and [SvelteKit3 migration](https://svelte.dev/docs/kit/migrating-to-sveltekit-3): supported framework conventions.

These references informed principles, not copied branding, wording, claims, packaging, photography or implementation. All application copy, layout, image generation and interface code are original to this fictional project. No website reference image is embedded as the working UI.

## System

Fraunces is the display family; Plus Jakarta Sans is the interface family. Both are self-hosted Latin WOFF2 assets with OFL notices and source URLs retained. Warm cream supports reading; sage gives the product stage its own field; amber enters through the original vessels and sensory still life. Fine rules organize the shop without turning every section into a rounded dashboard card.

Responsive product and ritual controls grow and wrap with enlarged text. Small-size price options stack at the narrowest width, rather than clipping amounts. Native inputs, radios, selects, details and dialogs carry the interactions. The site respects reduced motion and uses no scroll hijacking.
