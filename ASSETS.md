# Assets

Original Elnuara product and editorial images are created specifically for this fictional November2026 application. Exact media provenance, native and delivery dimensions, prompts and hashes are in docs/IMAGE_PROVENANCE.md and docs/image-prompts.json, maintained by the media owner. No prior-brand artwork is reused.

Fraunces and Plus Jakarta Sans are self-hosted Latin WOFF2 families. Their SIL Open Font License notices are in docs/licenses; original Google Fonts source URLs are retained in docs/FONT_SOURCES.css. Retrieved from official fonts.gstatic.com delivery and the google/fonts repository on November3,2026. The code MIT license does not replace third-party font licenses or assert exclusive rights in generated imagery.

The small Elnuara leaf mark and interface SVG glyphs are original code.
